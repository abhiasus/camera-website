Photography Tips

Learn. Practice. Improve.

Portrait Photography

Use natural light and focus on the subject's eyes. A simple background can make your subject stand out.

Landscape Photography

Use a smaller aperture and look for interesting foreground elements to create depth.

Night Photography

Use a tripod, lower your ISO when possible and experiment with longer shutter speeds.

Wildlife Photography

Use a fast shutter speed and keep enough distance from the subject.

Street Photography

Look for interesting moments, patterns and expressions around you.

Camera Settings

Understand ISO, aperture and shutter speed to gain better control over your photographs.
